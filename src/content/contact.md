Indiko is run by Kieran Klukas. Email is the fastest way to reach a human, and everything below is monitored by the same person.

## general and account questions

For questions about this instance, an account on it, or an app that integrates with it, email **hello@dunkirk.sh**. Accounts on `indiko.dunkirk.sh` are invite-only, so if you are asking for access you will need someone who already has an account to invite you.

## security reports

For anything that looks like a vulnerability, email **security@dunkirk.sh**. Please include the affected endpoint, the request you sent, and what you observed. The machine-readable contact details are published at [`/.well-known/security.txt`](/.well-known/security.txt), and the disclosure policy lives in [`SECURITY.md`](https://tangled.org/@dunkirk.sh/indiko/blob/main/SECURITY.md) in the repository.

Reports are answered as quickly as one person reasonably can. Please do not test against other people's accounts, and please do not run automated scanners that generate load on a small server.

## bugs and feature requests

Bugs and feature requests belong in the issue tracker of the [canonical repository](https://tangled.org/@dunkirk.sh/indiko). Include the version or commit you are running, the endpoint involved, and the full error response if there is one. Patches are welcome.
